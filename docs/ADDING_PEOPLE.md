## Image Requirements

### What Makes a Good Profile Image

When adding a person to the platform, the profile image is one of the most important elements. Here's what you need to know:

#### Technical Requirements
- **Format**: JPEG, PNG, or other common image formats
- **Size**: Images will be automatically compressed and resized to a maximum of 800x800 pixels
- **File Size**: While there's no strict limit, we recommend images under 5MB for faster uploads
- **Aspect Ratio**: Any aspect ratio works - the system maintains the original proportions

#### Content Guidelines
1. **Clear and Recognizable**: The image should clearly show the person's face
2. **Professional Quality**: Use high-resolution images that aren't blurry or pixelated
3. **Appropriate Content**: Images should be respectful and appropriate for all audiences
4. **Copyright**: Only use images you have permission to use or that are in the public domain
5. **Recent Photo**: Prefer current or well-known photos of the person

#### What to Avoid
- ❌ Blurry or low-quality images
- ❌ Group photos where the person is hard to identify
- ❌ Images with heavy filters or excessive editing
- ❌ Copyrighted material without permission
- ❌ Inappropriate or offensive content

#### Image Optimization
The platform automatically optimizes images by:
- Converting RGBA images to RGB format
- Resizing to a maximum of 800x800 pixels while maintaining aspect ratio
- Compressing to JPEG format with 85% quality
- This ensures fast loading times while maintaining visual quality

## Writing Descriptions

### Purpose of Descriptions

A good description helps users understand who the person is and why they might be interested in rating or learning more about them.

### Description Structure

#### 1. Opening Statement (1-2 sentences)
Start with the most important information:
- Who is this person?
- What are they best known for?

**Example**: 
> "Leonardo DiCaprio is an American actor and environmental activist. He gained recognition for his roles in films like Titanic, Inception, and The Revenant, for which he won an Academy Award."

#### 2. Key Achievements (2-4 sentences)
Highlight their most significant accomplishments:
- Major awards or recognitions
- Notable projects or contributions
- Career milestones

**Example**:
> "Throughout his career, DiCaprio has been nominated for seven Academy Awards and won Best Actor for The Revenant in 2016. He has collaborated with acclaimed directors such as Martin Scorsese, Christopher Nolan, and Quentin Tarantino. His performances in films like The Wolf of Wall Street and Django Unchained have received critical acclaim."

#### 3. Additional Context (1-2 sentences)
Add relevant information that gives more depth:
- Current activities
- Other notable areas of work
- Cultural impact

**Example**:
> "Beyond acting, DiCaprio is a dedicated environmental activist and has produced several documentaries on climate change. He established the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation in 1998 to support environmental causes."

### Description Guidelines

**Do:**
- ✅ Keep it objective and factual
- ✅ Use clear, concise language
- ✅ Focus on verifiable achievements
- ✅ Include recent information when relevant
- ✅ Write 100-300 words (longer for more prominent figures)
- ✅ Proofread for spelling and grammar

**Don't:**
- ❌ Include personal opinions or biases
- ❌ Use sensational or clickbait language
- ❌ Add unverified information or rumors
- ❌ Copy-paste from Wikipedia or other sources (paraphrase instead)
- ❌ Include excessive detail about personal life
- ❌ Use promotional language

### Writing Style
- Use third person (he/she/they)
- Present tense for current activities, past tense for completed actions
- Professional but accessible tone
- Avoid jargon unless necessary

## Using Tags

### What Are Tags?

Tags are comma-separated keywords that help users find and categorize people. They enable powerful search and filtering functionality.

### Tag Guidelines

#### Format
- Tags should be entered as comma-separated values
- Example: `actor, producer, environmentalist, oscar winner`
- Tags are case-insensitive and will be processed automatically
- Spaces around commas are automatically trimmed

#### Types of Tags to Include

1. **Profession/Role Tags**
   - Primary occupation: actor, director, musician, athlete, politician
   - Example: `actor, producer, filmmaker`

2. **Industry Tags**
   - The field they work in: entertainment, sports, technology, science
   - Example: `hollywood, film industry, entertainment`

3. **Achievement Tags**
   - Major awards or recognitions: oscar winner, nobel laureate, grammy winner
   - Example: `academy award winner, golden globe winner`

4. **Specialty Tags**
   - Specific areas of expertise or genre: action movies, comedy, drama, method acting
   - Example: `dramatic actor, action star, character actor`

5. **Era/Generation Tags**
   - Time period of prominence: 90s, 2000s, contemporary, classic
   - Example: `contemporary, 21st century`

6. **Additional Descriptors**
   - Other relevant characteristics: activist, philanthropist, author
   - Example: `environmental activist, humanitarian, author`

### Tag Best Practices

**Do:**
- ✅ Use 5-10 relevant tags
- ✅ Include the most important/searchable tags first
- ✅ Use commonly understood terms
- ✅ Be specific and accurate
- ✅ Use singular form when possible (actor vs actors)
- ✅ Include both general and specific tags

**Don't:**
- ❌ Overload with too many tags (20+)
- ❌ Use very obscure or made-up tags
- ❌ Duplicate the same concept (film actor, movie actor, cinema actor)
- ❌ Include tags that aren't relevant
- ❌ Use tags for opinions (great, best, overrated)

## Categories

### Selecting the Right Category

When adding a person, you'll need to select a category from the available options. Choose the category that best represents the person's primary area of recognition.

**Tips:**
- If the person is known for multiple areas, choose the one they're most famous for
- Categories help organize the platform and improve browsing
- You can use tags to capture additional areas of expertise

## Best Practices

### Before Adding a Person

1. **Search First**: Check if the person already exists on the platform
2. **Gather Information**: Have reliable sources ready for the description
3. **Find a Good Image**: Locate an appropriate, high-quality image before starting

### Quality Checklist

Before submitting, verify:
- ☑ Name is spelled correctly
- ☑ Description is factual, well-written, and proofread
- ☑ Image is clear, appropriate, and properly shows the person
- ☑ Category is correctly selected
- ☑ Tags are relevant, well-chosen, and properly formatted
- ☑ All required fields are filled out

### After Adding

- Your submission will be visible to all users immediately
- Other users can rate and comment on the person you added
- You can edit your submission later if you need to make corrections
- Be open to feedback from the community
